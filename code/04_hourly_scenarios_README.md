# Hourly curtailment and data center operating scenarios

Aggregates renewable generation by province, calibrates hourly wind and solar curtailment to reported utilization rates, and evaluates constant high-load, demand-following and curtailment-following operation. It estimates installed capacity and grid-related emissions and sweeps curtailment utilization targets from 10% to 100%.

## Inputs

County hourly generation files, a county-to-province mapping, provincial electricity load and utilization records, and the typical inference demand profile.

## Outputs

Hourly curtailment and operating-scenario datasets, annual provincial curtailment, installed capacity and scenario summaries.
